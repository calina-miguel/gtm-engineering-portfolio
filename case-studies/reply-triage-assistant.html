Reply Triage Assistant

A reply management concept that classifies inbound responses by intent, objection, timing, referral path, and recommended next action.

Problem

Positive replies get buried, objections are handled inconsistently, and follow-up timing often depends on manual discipline.

Workflow

Classify replies, detect sentiment, draft the next response, assign owner, and update CRM status.

Output

An inbox queue grouped by priority with recommended replies and suggested follow-up dates.

Client use

Useful for outbound teams handling direct messages, email replies, and founder-led sales inboxes.
