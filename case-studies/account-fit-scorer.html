ICP scoring

Account Fit Scorer

A scoring framework for ranking target accounts by ICP match, urgency, trigger signals, channel fit, and likely objection profile.

Problem

Outbound lists often treat every company the same, which wastes personalization effort on low-fit accounts.

Workflow

Score accounts across firmographic fit, recent hiring, buying intent, technology clues, and segment-specific pain.

Output

A prioritized account table with a recommended angle and confidence score for each prospect.

Tools to connect

Clay, Apollo, sales intelligence databases, Clearbit-style enrichment, CRM fields, and custom scoring rules.
