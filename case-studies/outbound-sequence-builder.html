Outbound Sequence Builder

A system concept that turns ICP pains, proof points, objections, and personalization inputs into a multi-touch email and direct outreach sequence.

Problem

Teams often use generic copy, but outbound needs sequence logic, channel timing, and objection handling.

Workflow

Input offer, buyer persona, trigger, proof, and CTA. Generate touch one, follow-up, objection reply, and bump message.

Output

A clean sequence draft with rationale for each step and editable personalization variables.

Client use

Useful for agencies, SDR teams, and founders who need repeatable outbound messaging systems.
