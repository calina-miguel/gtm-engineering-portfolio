Prospect research

Prospect Research Console

A research workflow for turning company pages, hiring signals, role data, tech stack clues, and public activity into usable personalization inputs.

Problem

Personalization is usually slow, shallow, or impossible to scale consistently.

Workflow

Collect account data, extract buyer context, identify trigger events, summarize pains, and generate a recommended message angle.

Output

A research card with source notes, sales angle, objection risk, and recommended first line.

Client use

Useful for building research ops behind founder-led sales, sales agencies, or SDR teams.
