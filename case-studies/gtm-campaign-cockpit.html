GTM Campaign Cockpit

An interactive workspace showing how a sales operation can move from business context to account scoring, outreach drafting, campaign launch, and reply triage.

Open workspace

Problem

Early-stage teams want outbound pipeline but often stitch together strategy, research, enrichment, copywriting, and inbox management manually. This workspace shows how those steps can become one guided GTM workflow.

What I built

A campaign cockpit with workflow status, KPI counters, prospect list generation, outreach preview, and reply classification.

Client value

Useful for founders or agencies that need a visual way to explain outbound operations before investing in a full automation stack.

Next version
